Echo360 Speed Keeper

Welcome

Stop re-toggling the speed menu every single lecture.

Echo360 forgets your playback speed the moment you close the tab. Echo360 Speed Keeper remembers it for you. Set your preferred speed once, and every recording you open plays at that speed automatically.

1

Click the action icon

Open any Echo360 recording and click the Echo360 Speed Keeper icon in your toolbar.

2

Drag the slider

Pick the speed that works for you. 1.5x is a popular starting point. The change applies instantly to the lecture playing in the tab behind this popup.

3

Forget about it

Every Echo360 recording you open from now on will play at this speed. The host player's own speed menu will follow along. You never have to touch this extension again.

Hi. I'm the solo developer behind Zovo. I built Echo360 Speed Keeper because I watched my partner re-toggle the speed menu on every lecture recording for an entire semester and finally snapped.

This extension is free, will stay free, and stores exactly one number on your device. No accounts. No cloud. No tracking. The lecture video stays in your browser tab the whole time.
